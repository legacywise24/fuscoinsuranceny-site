Attendance Requirements — Please Read Before You Enroll

Attendance in this live online class is an active eligibility requirement, not a formality. Students must:

  • Attend the entire course
  • Participate as required (segment check-ins, polls, and chat responses are attendance records)
  • Complete every required attendance verification — at the start of class, on return from break (within 5 minutes of the class resuming), and at the end of class (within 5 minutes of the class ending)
  • Meet the admission deadline — admission closes at 9:15 AM ET, 15 minutes after the class starts

Failure to meet these requirements may result in removal from the class, no course credit, and no certificate eligibility. Once you are admitted to the class, the fee is fully earned.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

If we cancel the course, you always get a full refund. If a class does not run for any reason on our side — including not reaching the minimum of 10 students, instructor emergency, or a technology failure on our side — you choose either a seat in any future class date or a full refund of your $59. If the course is not conducted, tuition is refunded in full.

If you cancel 48 hours or more before your class, you get a full refund. Cancel at least 48 hours before your class start time (9:00 AM ET on your class date) — call (929) 244-0744 or email mfusco@fuscoinsuranceny.com — and your $59 is refunded in full.

If you cancel less than 48 hours before your class, you get a credit, not a refund. Inside 48 hours, your $59 becomes a credit toward a future class date. A no-show is treated the same way: a credit toward a future date.

Once you are admitted to the class, the fee is fully earned. This course requires full attendance to earn a certificate: if you leave early, are removed under the attendance rules above, or do not complete the attendance verifications, no certificate can be issued and no refund is due.

Refunds go back the way you paid. Refunds are returned to your original payment method within 3 business days of cancellation, and we confirm your refund by email. We never ask for card details by phone, email, text, or chat.

Course materials and your Zoom link arrive the day before class. They are sent only to confirmed, paid students in the reminder sent the day before your class date — never at registration, and the Zoom link is never published on this website. Admission on class day is checked against the paid roster, one by one, beginning at 8:30 AM.
