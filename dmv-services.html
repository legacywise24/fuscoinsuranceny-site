What We Handle

We are a service bureau, not a DMV office: we prepare your paperwork correctly, file it with the Department of Motor Vehicles, and return your completed documents to you. Service fees are quoted up front before we begin. DMV fees are charged at cost and shown separately on your receipt — you always see exactly what DMV charges and what our service costs.

Obtaining Plates

New and replacement license plates secured at DMV on your behalf, including plate surrenders with an FS-6T receipt when you are taking a vehicle off the road.

Transfer of Vehicle

Registration and title transfer when you buy, sell, or gift a vehicle — paperwork prepared and filed end to end, including an out-of-state vehicle coming into a New York registration and title.

Sales Tax Filing

The New York sales tax paperwork for your vehicle purchase, prepared and filed together with your registration or transfer so the numbers are right the first time.

Duplicate Title

Lost or damaged New York title? We prepare and file your duplicate title application with DMV.

Duplicate Registration

Replacement registration documents prepared and filed when yours are lost, damaged, or never arrived.

Road Test Appointment

We schedule your New York road test appointment and confirm the details with you — what to bring, where to go, and when to be there.

How It Works

Good To Know

Our bureau handles New York motor vehicle transactions only. We prepare and file paperwork at the Department of Motor Vehicles — plates, registrations, and titles are issued by DMV, not in our office. Pairing your registration with your insurance is easy here: the same office that insures your vehicle can handle its DMV paperwork.

Fusco Insurance, Retirement & Wealth Planning Services Inc is a licensed Private Service Bureau but is not an official agency of the Department of Motor Vehicles, State of New York.

Payment & Refund Policy

Quoted before you pay. Every transaction is quoted up front, line by line: our service fee on one line, and the DMV fees and any sales tax — collected at cost and remitted to the DMV — on their own lines. Payment is due before we file. Paying your quote accepts it.

What the service fee covers. Our fee pays for preparing and filing your paperwork with the DMV. Approval, issuance, and processing times are the DMV's alone and are never guaranteed.

Service-fee refunds. If you cancel before preparation begins, the service fee is refunded in full. If you cancel after preparation but before filing, half the service fee is retained for the work performed and the balance is refunded. Once your paperwork has been filed with the DMV, the service fee is fully earned and is not refundable.

DMV fees and sales tax. Amounts not yet remitted to the DMV are refunded in full. Once remitted, any refund — for example a registration refund — is paid by the DMV directly to the registrant, and we will help with that paperwork. A duplicate or erroneous charge is always refunded in full.

If a payment is reversed. A reversed or disputed payment does not cancel amounts owed for services already rendered or government fees already advanced on your behalf.

Questions about a charge? Call us at (929) 244-0744 within 30 days of payment. We will pull your file — your signed terms, itemized quote, numbered receipt, and DMV filing proof — and correct any error of ours in full. Full terms are provided and signed at intake, before any payment is collected.
