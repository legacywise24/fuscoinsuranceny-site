The Course

Every class is the same New York PIRP course: a 6-hour course, taught live online via Zoom, 9:00 AM–3:00 PM ET, for $59 per student. Classes are held with a minimum of 10 students and a maximum of 40 students.

Up To 4 Points Reduced

Complete the course and up to 4 points are reduced from your New York driving record.

10% Off Auto Insurance — 3 Years

Completion gives you 10% off your auto insurance premiums for 3 years. Present your certificate to your insurer to start the discount.

Live Instructor on Zoom

A real instructor and a real class, with time for your questions — not a video you watch alone. Sign-in opens at 8:30 AM and admission is checked against the paid roster.

How Enrollment Works

1. Pick your date. Class dates are listed on the Class Schedule & Enrollment Calendar.

2. Request enrollment. Call (929) 244-0744 for your date. We confirm your seat and take your enrollment — $59 per student.

3. Get your materials the day before. Course materials and your Zoom link are sent only to confirmed, paid students in the reminder sent the day before your class date.

4. Take the class and get your certificate. Attend the full class and complete the attendance verifications. The sponsoring agency issues your completion certificate and reports your completion to DMV.

Company & Fleet Group Classes

Private classes for your drivers — up to 40 per class. We schedule a group class for your company or fleet on a date arranged for your group. Each driver who completes the course earns the same point reduction and insurance discount on their own record and policy, and your business gets a documented, approved safety course for every driver who completes it.

Good To Know

Courses are delivered through Defensive Drivers Discount — New York Defensive Driver's Discount, LLC, a New York State DMV-approved sponsoring agency, Sponsor #35 — which owns the approved course, issues your completion certificate, and reports your completion to DMV. Fusco Insurance is not the course sponsor, and certificates are not issued by Fusco. A course does not dismiss a ticket or remove a suspension. Attendance and cancellation terms — including the 9:15 AM admission close, the attendance verifications, the 48-hour refund rule, and the day-before delivery of materials and the Zoom link — are stated in full on the Class Schedule & Enrollment Calendar.
